Admissions

We welcome families interested in joining Olanipekun International Academy. Here is an overview of the application process.

Application process

  1. Contact the school office to ask about available places and entry requirements.
  2. Complete an admission application and provide the requested documents.
  3. Attend any assessment or meeting arranged by the school.
  4. Follow the school's instructions to confirm an offer and complete enrolment.

Documents to prepare

  • Recent school reports or academic records
  • Birth certificate or other proof of age
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Parent or guardian contact information
